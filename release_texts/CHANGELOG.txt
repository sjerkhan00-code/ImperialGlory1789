IMPERIAL GLORY 1789 - CHANGELOG

1.0.0 - first public release
* 1789 world campaign: Europe, Asia and Africa, 280 provinces, natural
  borders, islands, new ports and a redrawn diplomacy map.
* 29 playable nations; seven regional peoples hold the rest of the world.
* 1789 borders, armies, fleets, technologies, alliances and wars.
* New uniforms, unit cards, flags, troop names and battlefields.
* Crash fixes for all non-empire nations; much faster AI turns.
* The camera starts on your capital, scrollable Select Empire, 'Skip all'
  for notices, longer queues, autosave every turn, 4K resolutions.
* Quick Game with every nation; optional Cheats menu (player only).
* Offline installer; no original game files in the download.
